### The short version

Most farm tokens die the same way: emissions outrun demand, price falls, yields
look worse, stakers leave, and the whole thing unwinds.

Shrooms has no emissions. SPORES is minted only against HYPE the treasury has
already received from SHROOMS trading fees, so supply is an output of real
activity rather than a policy. Every SPORES is redeemable for a slice of that
treasury, and the redemption floor can never fall — it is asserted on chain.

Spending SPORES on in-game boosts burns it and leaves the HYPE behind. The
players who play make the token worth more for the players who leave.
